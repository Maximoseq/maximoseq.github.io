Bicycle Traffic on Seattle's Fremont Bridge

R, tidyverse, lubridate, ggplot2

Question
How has bike traffic on the bridge changed over time, and how do commuters compare to weekend riders?
What I did
Analyzed 112,000+ hourly counts from Seattle Open Data (2012 to 2025) in R, built the charts in ggplot2, and wrote up the data's limitations. I kept working on it after the class ended and turned it into a full report.
Result
Crossings grew steadily through 2019, then dropped sharply in 2020. Weekend traffic fell less than weekday traffic, which points to commuting as the main change.
