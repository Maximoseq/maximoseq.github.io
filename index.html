A team security review of a deliberately insecure password manager.

Problem

What I did
I researched and presented the authentication, authorization, and hardening sections of our final presentation. Bicycle Traffic on Seattle's Fremont Bridge

R, tidyverse, lubridate, ggplot2

An R analysis of hourly bike counts on the Fremont Bridge from 2012 to 2025.

More info
Question
How has bike traffic on the bridge changed over time, and how do commuters compare to weekend riders?
What I did
Analyzed 112,000+ hourly counts from Seattle Open Data (2012 to 2025) in R, built the charts in ggplot2, and wrote up the data's limitations. I kept working on it after the class ended and turned it into a full report.
Result
Crossings grew steadily through 2019, then dropped sharply in 2020. Weekend traffic fell less than weekday traffic, which points to commuting as the main change.

Device Repurposing Analysis for Intel

SQL (joins, CTEs, CASE, aggregation), SQLPad

A Global Career Accelerator case project using a simulated dataset modeled on Intel's device repurposing program.

More info
Problem
Intel wanted to know which repurposed devices to prioritize for the biggest drop in energy use and CO₂ emissions.
What I did
Joined device and impact data for about 600,000 laptops and desktops in SQL, then grouped devices by age, type, and region using CASE and CTEs.
Result
Older devices saved about 48 kWh a year each, compared with about 19 kWh for newer ones, but made up only about 3% of the program. Devices in Asia saved the most CO₂, about 15.5 kg each, compared with 6.4 kg in Europe. I recommended a trade-in program to bring in more older devices, starting in Asia.
