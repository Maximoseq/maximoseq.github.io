How I work

Design & Product

I focus on understanding how people interact with systems and where those systems break down. That means product design, UX, and systems thinking, with an emphasis on real-world constraints, tradeoffs, and data-informed decisions.

Data & Analysis

I use data to explain what's happening, not just what we assume is happening. I primarily use R for visualization and exploratory analysis, and I'm using Python more in my coursework to expand how I work with and visualize data, aiming for clean analysis, readable visuals, and straightforward interpretation.
