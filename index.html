Bicycle Traffic Patterns on Seattle's Fremont Bridge

Problem

Seattle counts bikes on the Fremont Bridge every hour, but the raw counts don't show how riding has changed.

What I did

Analyzed 112,440 hourly bike-count records from 2012 to 2025 in R and split them into weekdays and weekends. I kept working on it after the class ended.

Result

Riding grew through 2019, then dropped sharply in 2020. Weekday totals fell by about 40% while weekend totals held steady.
